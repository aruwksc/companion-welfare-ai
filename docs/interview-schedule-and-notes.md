# Participant Interview Schedule & Anonymized Notes (CWA-9, CWA-14, CWA-15)

## Scheduling & Anonymization Protocol (CWA-9)
- **Participants**: 5 dog owners / caretakers assigned anonymized IDs `P1` through `P5` (no personally identifiable information stored in repository).
- **Guide & Consent**: Conducted under the consent checklist and semi-structured interview guide (`docs/consent-and-interview-guide.md`, `CWA-5`).
- **Synthesis Link**: Findings consolidated into `docs/interview-hypothesis-synthesis.md` (`CWA-21`).

## Anonymized Session Notes (`P2`–`P5`)

### Participant P2 (CWA-14)
- **Context**: Multi-dog household; records short clips when dogs are left alone or resting.
- **Key Observations**: Strongly prefers local desktop storage over cloud camera subscriptions; wants an explicit consent toggle before any file is processed (`SYS-01`, `SYS-02`).
- **Workflow Needs**: Timeline jump to specific timestamps helps verify restless pacing vs. normal settling down.

### Participant P3 (CWA-15)
- **Context**: First-time dog owner tracking post-adoption adjustment and separation behavior.
- **Key Observations**: Worried about apps giving alarming "medical" or "anxiety" diagnoses; prefers neutral, observable descriptions with clear non-diagnostic disclaimers (`SYS-06`).
- **Workflow Needs**: Wants to attach personal context notes (`is_manual`) to specific moments (e.g., loud hallway noise outside).

### Participant P4 (CWA-15)
- **Context**: Owner working with a positive-reinforcement trainer.
- **Key Observations**: Finds automated event detection helpful as a starting point, but still reviews clips manually before sharing summaries (`SYS-03`..`SYS-05`).
- **Workflow Needs**: Clean, portable Markdown/text summary export to share observations with a trainer without sending raw multi-gigabyte videos.

### Participant P5 (CWA-14)
- **Context**: Caretaker of a senior dog monitoring daily rest and mobility patterns.
- **Key Observations**: Values offline persistence across sessions (`SQLite`) so historical observations remain organized on one machine (`SYS-04`).
- **Workflow Needs**: Simple single-window layout separating profile/consent, video timeline, and report preview.
