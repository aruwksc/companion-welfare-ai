# Consent Form & Semi-Structured Interview Guide (CWA-5)

## Participant Informed Consent Checklist
- **Purpose of Study**: Understand dog owners' and reviewers' workflows, privacy expectations, and non-diagnostic behavioral review needs for Companion Welfare AI.
- **Data Handling & Privacy**: Personal notes and interview responses are anonymized (`P1`..`P5`); local video files and SQLite records remain under participant control with explicit consent (`consent_given`).
- **Non-Diagnostic Notice**: System outputs and discussion materials provide behavioral observation support and do not constitute veterinary medical diagnosis.
- **Voluntary Participation**: Participants may skip questions or withdraw consent at any time.

## Semi-Structured Interview Guide
1. **Current Routine & Monitoring**: How do you currently observe your dog's daily behavior, rest, or stress cues at home?
2. **Prior Tools & Limitations**: Have you tried cameras, wearables, or notes? What worked well and what felt limiting or intrusive?
3. **Privacy & Consent Expectations**: What matters most to you regarding video storage, local processing, and permission/consent controls?
4. **Timeline Review & Manual Notes**: How helpful is jumping to specific timestamps and adding your own manual notes alongside automated detections?
5. **Report Clarity & Non-Diagnostic Framing**: How should summary notes/reports be phrased so their non-diagnostic nature is clear and easy to share?
